Sizing, readability & native materials · revision 3

13px/18px primary type, 11–12px/16–18px metadata and 24–28px counters. Regular text does most of the work; medium and semibold identify actions and key numbers. System sans labels use tabular numbers, and collapsed routing reserves a constant width across 0%–100% so the slider and expand button never shift in either language. Instrument reserves monospace for its main counters.

Spacing uses 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32px tokens. Related details sit 4–8px apart; major information groups sit 16px apart. Expanded panels remain 360px wide (320px content column) and include a compact static demo NextReset signal block inspired by nextreset.ai (96% next-24h estimated probability beside announced/rollout state, 1h 16m window countdown, and 48h/72h horizons). Collapsed panels are 300 × 94px. Hit areas stay at least 32px high, informed by Apple HIG and Carbon spacing.

In the native macOS app, Liquid Glass uses Apple’s native NSGlassEffectView plus native text-local blur and Frosted Glass uses NSVisualEffectView behind a non-opaque floating panel, with automatic solid fallbacks when Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast is enabled. In this offline HTML preview, Liquid Glass uses a single aggregate canvas-generated Gaussian alpha mask derived from tight DOM text Range.getClientRects() bounds (alpha = 0.96 * (1 - exp(-density)) with moderate 1.25× Gaussian spread and the whole frost group capped at 70% and 16px backdrop blur, leaving empty panel regions and corners clear and invariant to focus/hover). Text colors are checked using WCAG relative luminance with a 4.5:1 minimum target.
